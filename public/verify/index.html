Public signing key

Every receipt in the local hash chain is signed with Ed25519 and linked to the previous receipt hash. The public key for fleet verification is:

ed25519:agentos-studio-fleet

How to verify locally

In AgentOS Studio, open Timeline or call the public_ledger and receipts_verify commands to check the SHA-256 chain and Ed25519 signatures against your local receipts.jsonl file, or fetch the live summary at /api/ledger.
